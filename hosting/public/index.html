Private by design

Your birthday isn't a public announcement

Candlewish was built so the people who know you can celebrate you, and nobody else can find you.

Only your circle sees it

Your birthday is shown only to people you're connected to. Strangers can't look you up.

Numbers are hashed

Contacts are matched using a one-way keyed hash. We never store your friends' numbers in plain text.

Block & report

Block anyone instantly, report anything that isn't kind, and delete any wish on your own wall.

Leave anytime

Delete your account from Settings and your profile, birthday and wishes go with it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Candlewish free?

Yes. Candlewish is free to download and use, and there are no ads inside the app.

Who can see my birthday?

Only people you're connected to: people you celebrate and people who celebrate you. Your city stays private to you, and nobody can find you without already having your number. Read the privacy policy.

Will my contacts get a message when I join?

No. We never message your contacts. Invites are only sent when you choose to share one yourself, for example on WhatsApp.

Why don't I see a friend's birthday yet?

Birthdays are set by each person themselves, so they're always accurate. Once your friend joins Candlewish and adds their birthday, it appears in your list. Invite them in one tap.

Is it available on iPhone?

Candlewish is launching on Android first. iPhone is on the way.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account. See what gets deleted, or how to request deletion without the app.
