Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Candlewish and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings icon.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete everything.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email sachinbansal091@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Candlewish account", sent from or mentioning the phone number you signed up with. We may ask you to confirm the request with a code sent to that number. We will delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in phone number.
  • Your profile: name, username, date of birth, city and profile photo.
  • Every wish and photo you posted, on anyone's wall.
  • Your list of people you celebrate, your blocks, reports you made, your notifications and reminder settings.
  • Your push notification tokens. Reminders about your birthday stop.

What may be kept

  • Friends who added you keep an entry in their list, renamed "Deleted user", with no birthday. Your number can no longer be matched to it.
  • Reports other people made about your content, including a copy of that content, for as long as needed to resolve them and meet legal obligations.
  • Analytics and crash data already sent to Google Firebase and Meta, kept under their retention settings (at most 14 months for analytics) and not linked to a Candlewish account any more.
  • Information we must keep by law.

Delete only some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove things. In the app you can delete any wish you posted or any wish on your own wall, remove your profile photo, and remove people from your list. For anything else, email us.
