The short version

  • You sign in with your phone number. Your birthday is shared only with people you're connected to, and your city only with you.
  • If you choose to import contacts, we check which numbers are on Candlewish. Numbers are stored only as a one-way keyed hash, never in plain text.
  • We use Google Firebase and Meta to understand how the app is used, fix crashes, and measure our ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

Candlewish ("we", "us") is an app for remembering birthdays and sending wishes. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Candlewish Android app and this website.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Phone number: to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Profile: your name, username, date of birth, city and (optionally) a profile photo.
  • Wishes: messages and photos you post on other people's walls.
  • Reports and blocks: when you report a wish or person, we store the report, the reason and a copy of the reported content. When you block someone, we store that block.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.

Your contacts (only if you allow it)

If you grant contacts permission, the app reads the phone numbers in your address book and sends them to our server to find out which of your contacts are on Candlewish. These numbers are not stored. For the contacts you choose to add, we store the name you saved for them and their phone number as a one-way keyed hash (HMAC-SHA-256), not the number itself. We never message your contacts unless you choose to send an invite yourself.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage events: actions such as signing up, importing contacts, sending an invite, posting a wish and scrolling the feed, with your account ID. Collected by Google Firebase Analytics and Meta (Facebook) App Events.
  • Crash reports: device model, OS version, app version and the state of the app at the time of a crash, with your account ID. Collected by Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Device identifiers: an advertising ID and app-instance ID used by Firebase and Meta, and a push notification token so we can send you reminders.
  • Ad measurement: to tell whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram led to installs, the Meta SDK receives your phone number, date of birth and city. The SDK hashes these on your device before sending them.

2. How we use your information

  • Run the app: sign you in, show your friends' upcoming birthdays, let people wish you on your birthday, and send reminders and notifications.
  • Show who you know: match your contacts to people already on Candlewish.
  • Keep Candlewish safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our Terms, and prevent abuse such as spam and SMS fraud.
  • Improve the app: understand which features are used and fix crashes.
  • Measure our advertising: learn which ads bring people to Candlewish.
  • Meet legal obligations.

We process your information on the basis of your consent, which you give by creating an account and granting permissions, and which you can withdraw at any time, and where needed to provide the service you asked for or to comply with law.

4. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run Candlewish, under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google Firebase: push notifications, analytics, crash reporting and remote configuration.
  • Meta Platforms: app events and ad measurement. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Fast2SMS: delivering sign-in codes by SMS.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of our users or the public, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

Some providers may process data on servers outside India. Where they do, we rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, wishes and photos: until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports: as long as needed to review them and meet legal obligations, even if the reported content is later deleted.
  • Analytics and crash data: under Firebase's and Meta's retention settings, at most 14 months for analytics.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your profile in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account in Settings → Delete account, or follow these steps.
  • Contacts: you can turn off contacts permission in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Notifications: switch reminders and wish notifications off in Settings, or in your phone's settings.
  • Ads personalisation: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Withdraw consent / complain: email us at the address below. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Not on Candlewish?

If a friend added your number, we store only a keyed hash of it and the name they saved for you, visible only to them. Email us your number and we will remove it and stop it from being added again.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data in our database is restricted by per-user rules. Phone numbers are stored only as keyed hashes, and photos are stored privately and served through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as the law requires.

8. Children

Candlewish is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

10. Contact and grievance officer

We aim to respond to privacy requests and grievances within 30 days.
